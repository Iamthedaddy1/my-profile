My teaching approach

Making technology easier to understand, one idea at a time.

I believe students learn best when they understand the why, get hands-on experience, and have the freedom to experiment. My classes combine clear explanations, practical projects, and guided problem-solving to turn curiosity into real skills.

Understand first

I break complex ideas into simple explanations students can relate to, building understanding before moving to the next concept.

Learn by doing

Students practise through coding exercises, creative projects, experiments, and activities that turn theory into experience.

Build confidence

I encourage questions, independent thinking, and learning from mistakes, helping students become more confident problem-solvers.

Make progress visible

From a first Scratch animation to a working website, I use achievable milestones to help students see what they can do and what to learn next.

My goal is not just to teach students how to use technology, but to help them understand it, create with it, and develop the confidence to solve problems on their own.
